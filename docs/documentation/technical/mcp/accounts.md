# `accounts` server

Skill `balance_inquiry`. Code: `app/adapters/inbound/mcp/accounts.py`, SQL in `app/adapters/outbound/postgres/accounts.py`, rules in `app/domain/accounts.py`. Common rules: [README.md](README.md).

## `get_balances(product_type?)`

Balances of the customer's products and their totals.

| Argument | Values |
|---|---|
| `product_type` (optional) | One of the eight product types, e.g. `"Tarjeta Crédito"`. Omitted: every product |

Returns:

- `products`: per product, `product_type`, `product_number_last4`, `currency`, `current_balance`, `credit_limit`, `product_status`, and for a credit card `credit_available` (limit minus balance, never below 0).
- `totals`: the Active products added up per group and currency. Groups: `deposits` (savings and checking), `investments`, `debt` (credit cards and loans). Debit cards are left out, because the data does not say which account they draw on.

## `get_debts(product_type?)`

What the customer owes on credit cards and loans, and when to pay.

| Argument | Values |
|---|---|
| `product_type` (optional) | `"Tarjeta Crédito"`, `"Préstamo Personal"` or `"Préstamo Hipotecario"` |

Returns `debts`, one per product: `debt` (balance owed), `interest_rate` (annual %), `days_past_due`, `statement_date`, `due_date`, `statement_balance` (cards), `minimum_payment` (cards: the minimum due, past due included; loans: the installment), `past_due_amount`, `remaining_installments` (loans). Plus `as_of` and `billing_provenance`.

The balance, rate and days past due are organizer data. The dates and payment amounts come from `core.billing`, generated by the team with a fixed rule ([data model](../data/model.md)), as of 2026-06-18. The skill tells the model to give them with that date and never as a payment the customer can make here.

## `get_profile()`

The customer's own data on record: `first_name`, `last_name`, `city`, `country`, `segment`, `preferred_language`, `customer_status`, `customer_since`, `tenure_years`, and `products` (how many of each type, and how many Active). No document number, email or phone is returned.

## Example

```json
{
  "as_of": "2026-06-18",
  "debts": [{
    "product_type": "Tarjeta Crédito", "product_number_last4": "2951", "currency": "MXN",
    "debt": 8100.0, "interest_rate": 36.0, "days_past_due": 0, "product_status": "Active",
    "statement_date": "2026-06-14", "due_date": "2026-07-04",
    "statement_balance": 6075.0, "minimum_payment": 500.0, "past_due_amount": 0.0
  }],
  "billing_provenance": "team_generated",
  "evidence_lookup": "get_debts"
}
```
